### Purpose

`bgfx::EncoderImpl::submit`: records the encoder's current draw into the Frame. It is stock bgfx `EncoderImpl::submit(ViewId, ProgramHandle, OcclusionQueryHandle, uint32_t depth, uint8_t flags)` with two Mojang additions (a cached hash of the bind block, and an optional per-draw side table) [V: all 324 instructions are accounted for in the reversed source below]. Every draw of the game goes through this function or one of its three siblings (0xbc61140, 0xbc61560, 0xbc618e0). Callers: 0x569a3a0, 0x56ad350, 0x56ad810, 0xbcc39b0, 0xbcc3b90, 0xbcc3e50, 0xbcc3fa0, 0xbcc4150, 0xbcc4b90, 0xc270330, 0xc295d50 [V].
