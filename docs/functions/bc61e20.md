### Purpose

`bgfx::Frame::sort()`: remaps the view id inside every draw sort key and every blit key from "view id" to "view order", then radix-sorts the draw keys (with their u16 item indices) and the blit keys. After it, the backend walks `m_sortValues` in order. It is byte-for-byte the shape of stock bgfx `Frame::sort` [V: every instruction of the 0x215-byte body is accounted for below]. Callers: D3D12 submit 0xe82e230 and the two other renderer submits 0xe7f5380, 0xe85e5c0 [V].
