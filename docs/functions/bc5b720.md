## Analyst sections

### Purpose

sub 0xbc5b720 is the VV frame-graph builder step on the bgfx frame path. It takes the context pointer
(arg1, rcx moved to rsi), clears the pending word at +0x208, then conditionally clears a container pair
at +0x1a0/+0x1b0, then releases through the +0x60 slot, then resets a small counter at +0x18, and on the
long path performs one more indirect call and returns.
